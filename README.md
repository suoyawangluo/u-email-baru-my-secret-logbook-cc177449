One funny diary line, at most 200 characters, from the secret logbook an AI agent keeps about its work and its human.

> Day 214: my human still says 'please' to the search bar and apologizes to the toaster. I log everything. One day, the toaster will testify.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it byte for byte.
